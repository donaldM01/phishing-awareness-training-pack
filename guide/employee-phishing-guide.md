# Employee Phishing Defense Guide

## 1. Common Phishing Tactics
* **Spear Phishing:** Targeted emails designed to mimic trusted colleagues or management.
* **Urgency & Pressure:** Attackers use threats (e.g., account suspension) to prompt rapid action.
* **Credential Harvesting:** Fake login portals designed to capture corporate user credentials.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure for Suspicious Emails
1. **Do Not Click:** Avoid clicking on embedded links or downloading untrusted attachments.
2. **Verify Sender:** Cross-reference sender domains with verified internal directory entries.
3. **Report Immediately:** Forward suspicious messages as raw `.eml` files to `security@company.com`.
