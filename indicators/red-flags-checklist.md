
# Phishing Indicators & Red Flags Checklist

- [ ] **Mismatch in Sender Address:** Display name matches an executive, but the domain differs (e.g., `ceo@company-support.com` vs `ceo@company.com`).
- [ ] **Generic Greetings:** Greetings like "Dear Customer" or "Dear Employee" instead of personalized names.
- [ ] **Mismatched URL Links:** Hovering over a hyperlink reveals a destination domain different from the displayed text.
- [ ] **Unexpected Attachments:** Archives (`.zip`, `.rar`), macro-enabled files (`.xlsm`), or executable scripts (`.iso`, `.exe`).
- [ ] **Grammar & Tone Inconsistencies:** Unusual syntax, spelling errors, or awkward formatting.
